# Shinobi Éternel II — v3.1 « Réaction Update »

Theme: element reactions. Nothing else changes.

## Marks
- Every elemental technique leaves a mark (炎 風 水 雷 土) above the enemy for 4.5 s. The icon has a ring showing the time left.
- A different element landing on a marked enemy triggers the reaction:
  - The reaction name pops up in large text, with a kanji, a shockwave and its own effects.
  - The reaction is also added to a feed at the top-left of the screen.

## Reaction table
| Pair | Reaction | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fire + Wind | Fire Storm | Fire tornado that spreads to nearby enemies and sets them ablaze |
| Water + Lightning | Electrocution | Electrified zone; everyone inside is paralysed |
| Earth + Water | Mire | Mud; enemies are heavily slowed |
| Fire + Water | Blinding Steam | Scalding steam that blinds |
| Lightning + Wind | Storm | Long-lasting storm cloud with random lightning strikes |
| Earth + Fire | Lava Flow | Lake of lava |
| Earth + Wind | Sandstorm | Blinds, slows, pushes back |
| Water + Wind | Blizzard | Rapid freezing |
| Fire + Lightning | Plasma | Area explosion |
| Lightning + Earth | Magnetism | Pulls nearby enemies together and stuns them |

## Dōjutsu, genjutsu and teamwork
- **Mirage:** a genjutsu on a marked target turns the mark into 2 s of terror.
- **Nightmare:** an element on a target under genjutsu deals +40 %, and the illusion lasts longer.
- **Sharingan:** reactions deal +30 % and put the target in a 1 s trance.
- **Byakugan:** reactions seal the target's chakra for 2.5 s.
- **Rinnegan:** reaction zones are ×1.6 wider and pull enemies to the centre.
- **Ally Combo:** triggering a mark laid by an ally gives +35 % and chakra to both of you.

## Enemies
- Status icons above every enemy (wet, paralysed, frozen, mired, blinded, trance, sealed, poison…).
- Resistant enemies (盾 icon) take −40 % from their element and cannot be marked.
- **Alchemists** (C-rank and higher deserters and illusionists) set up reactions under your feet. A warning circle appears first: get out of it.
- From A-rank, some enemies take half damage from reactions.

## UI and tutorial
- New **Réactions** tab in the village: element grid, every reaction with its discovery count, and a status-icon legend.
- 2 new tutorial steps (20 in total): triggering a reaction, and dodging an Alchemist.

## Fixes

- Save compatible with v3.0.
